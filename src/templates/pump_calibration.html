Pump Flow Rate Calibration

Gravimetric calibration of the relay-switched pump. The two-constant model V = Q × (ton − τ) separates the true flow rate Q from the per-actuation dead time τ (relay pull-in, spin-up, line pressurisation) charged on every dose.

Setup

Prime until the line is visibly bubble-free (bubbles are the biggest error source). Plumb exactly as for a real titration: same tubing, length, outlet height and back-pressure. Use distilled water and an analytical balance (≥ 0.001 g).

Stage 0 — Bootstrap

Three continuous runs (5, 10, 15 s). Over these durations the dead time contributes < 0.2%, so Qrough = Σmass / (ρ × 30) is good to ~1% — enough to size the duration series.

Place the tared beaker under the outlet before each run. Weigh and record after each run, then re-tare.

Auxiliary Checks

Two checks the fit cannot make. Both are advisory — a failure means the two-constant model needs qualifying, not that the fit is wrong.

A — Burst-length check

Same on-time ( s) at N = 10, 40, 100. Volume per pulse should be identical; if small-N runs come out low there is a per-run priming penalty on top of τ, and calibration must be done at a burst length close to the real titration.

B — Gap check

Same on-time and N () at gaps 0.2 s and 5 s. If they differ, the pump is still coasting at the short gap and the calibration is only valid at the gap it was measured with.
